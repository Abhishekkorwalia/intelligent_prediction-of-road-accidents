About the System

This system uses advanced machine learning techniques to predict the severity of road accidents based on various environmental and situational factors. The predictions help in:

  • Emergency response planning and resource allocation
  • Identifying high-risk conditions for preventive measures
  • Understanding patterns in accident severity
  • Supporting policy decisions for road safety

Severity Levels

Minor

Moderate

Minor injuries requiring medical attention

Severe

Serious injuries, hospitalization required

Fatal
